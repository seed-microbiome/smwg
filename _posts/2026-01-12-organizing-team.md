<h4>As we begin 2026, we are pleased to share an update regarding the coordination of the Seed Microbiome Working Group (SMWG).</h4>

<p>Since its foundation, the SMWG has grown into an active and diverse community of early career researchers interested in all aspects of seed microbiome research.</p> 

<p>To support the continued growth and activities of the SMWG, the group is now coordinated by an organising team composed of Gillian Bergmann, Cristian Salinas-Castillo, and Carolina Lobato (co-founder). Together, the organising team will oversee and support SMWG initiatives, including community meetings and collaborative activities.</p> 

 <p>We look forward to continuing to foster collaboration, discussion, and knowledge exchange within the seed microbiome community.</p>
