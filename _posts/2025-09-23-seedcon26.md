<h2>SeedCon 2026 – The First International Conference on the Seed Microbiome and Microbial Inheritance will take place May 5–7, 2026, at the Leibniz Institute for Agricultural Engineering and Bioeconomy (ATB) in Potsdam, Germany.

This event will bring together leading scientists, industry experts, and stakeholders to discuss the seed microbiome, its role in plant health and productivity, and the latest research on microbial inheritance. 

Abstract submission will open soon. More information at <a href="https://www.seedcon.org/">seedcon.org</a></h2>
