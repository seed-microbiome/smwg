<h2>We are proud to share that both our co-founder Makrina Diakaki, and Simone Bosco, presented their research at the IOBC-WPRS XVII Meeting for Biological and Integrated Control of Plant Pathogens, and were awarded the IOBC-WPRS Award in recognition of their outstanding work and contributions to her field.

In the closing speech of the conference, the organizers highlighted the Seed Microbiome Working Group as an inspiring example of a young researcher–led initiative, emphasizing the importance of such communities for the advancement of science. Visit <a href="https://www.iobctorino2025.org/">iobctorino2025.org</a> for more details!</h2>
