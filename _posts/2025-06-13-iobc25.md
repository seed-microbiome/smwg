<br>
<p>We are proud to share that both our co-founder Makrina Diakaki, and Simone Bosco, presented their research at the IOBC-WPRS XVII Meeting for Biological and Integrated Control of Plant Pathogens, and were awarded the IOBC-WPRS Award in recognition of their outstanding work and contributions to her field.</p>

<p>In the closing speech of the conference, the organizers highlighted the Seed Microbiome Working Group as an inspiring example of a young researcher–led initiative, emphasizing the importance of such communities for the advancement of science.</p>

<p>Visit <strong><a href="https://www.iobctorino2025.org/">iobctorino2025.org</a></strong> for more details.</p>
