<h1>Looking Ahead - The Start of the SMWG</h1>

<br>
<h2>On Thursday, October 13th, 2022, we took the first step in building a vibrant community of seed microbiome researchers with the launch of the SMWG. This group was founded with a clear vision: to foster collaboration, exchange expertise, and create lasting connections among early-career scientists. Through presentations, discussions, and shared insights, we aim to advance our field together, bridging knowledge gaps and shaping the future of seed microbiome research </h2>
