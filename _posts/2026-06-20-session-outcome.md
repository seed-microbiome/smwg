<br>
<p>The Seed Microbiome Working Group (SMWG) was delighted to host a dynamic and highly interactive session at SeedCon early May titled <em>Shaping the Future of Seed Microbiome Research</em>. The session brought together researchers at different career stages in a collaborative World Café–style format, fostering open discussion, idea exchange, and community building around some of the most pressing questions in seed microbiome research.</p> 

<p>One of the most valuable outcomes of the session was the way it connected early-career researchers and senior scientists in a collaborative setting. The exchange of perspectives helped surface new ideas, strengthen ongoing discussions, and highlight shared challenges across different systems and approaches. The energy in the room reflected a growing and increasingly cohesive community working toward a common goal: advancing our understanding of seed microbiomes and their applications.</p> 

<h4>Overall, the session was a great success. It not only generated numerous insightful ideas but also contributed to building stronger links within the SMWG network and the broader research community. We are grateful to all participants and facilitators who contributed their time, expertise, and enthusiasm.</h4>

<p>We look forward to continuing these discussions and building on the momentum created at SeedCon as we collectively shape the future of seed microbiome research.</p>
