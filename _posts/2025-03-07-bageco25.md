<h4>BAGECO 2025 takes place in Graz this July and you can still submit your abstract until the 14th of March!</h4>

<br>
<p>The BAGECO conference in bacterial genetics and ecology will cover diverse topics, including microbial community assembly, plant-microbe interactions, microbiome functions, and microbial adaptation to environmental changes. Visit <a href="https://bageco.org">bageco.org</a> for more details.</p>
